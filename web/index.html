PROPOSED ALGORITHMIC COMPOSITION

RECAP: resolve uncertainty before hiding it

Recovery-conditioned Ensemble with Cost-Aware Probing

01 / CONDITION

The EHR sets the starting point

Personal resting HR/RR, peak-flow best, and past attacks condition the measurement model and hypothesis priors.

02 / COMPETE

Nine small virtual patients

Three explanations × three recovery-retention settings. Each tracks obstruction proxy, trend, recovery debt and sensor bias.

03 / LEARN STATE

Update with the observed stream

Kalman updates estimate continuous states. Tempered evidence and soft forgetting update hypothesis weights. New observations can change the explanation.

04 / ASK

Spend only for expected information

Gaussian quadrature estimates risk-index and hypothesis entropy reduction for an optional peak-flow or independent RR observation. Subtract assumed burden; enforce a two-probe budget.

The measurement objective

score(a) = U(now) − Ey | a[ U(after y) ] − burden(a)

U = entropy of the mixture’s Bernoulli risk index + 0.15 × entropy of hypothesis weights.

The planner holds component risk indices fixed while updating hypothetical hypothesis weights. It approximates information value; it is not exact full-state Bayesian decision theory. A wait action wins when no positive score remains.

Design contribution

RECAP integrates personal recovery dynamics, competing airway and sensor explanations, and a limited observation budget into one inspectable state-update workflow.

The composition builds on established Kalman filtering, Bayesian model averaging and information-gain selection. Related-work notes and reproducible ablations document its foundations, measured benefits and remaining research questions.
